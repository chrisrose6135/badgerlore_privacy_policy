# Privacy Policy — Badgerlore the Edgelord

**Last updated:** 13 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how **Badgerlore the Edgelord** (the “Game”) handles information when played on Android. The Game is distributed through Google Play and may also be distributed through Steam.

## Game accounts and personal information

The Game does not require you to register an account with the game developer or provide a name, email address, telephone number or location to play.

The Game is designed as a single-player experience. Its gameplay does not depend on creating a developer-operated online profile.

## Saved games and settings

The Game stores saved progress, achievements and preferences locally on your device. These records support save slots and gameplay settings; they are not intended to be transmitted to the game developer.

You can delete individual save slots using the Game’s save management controls. You can also remove local app data using your device’s operating-system settings. Uninstalling the Game may delete local data, subject to your platform's backup and restore behaviour.

## Advertising and analytics

The current Game is not designed to display third-party advertisements or to use developer-operated behavioural analytics.

**Publication check:** The developer must confirm the production release does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other SDK that collects information before relying on this statement for Google Play’s Data safety declaration.

## Platform services

When you download, install or play the Game through a platform such as Google Play or Steam, that platform may process information (for example, purchases, downloads, device diagnostics and account information) according to its own terms and privacy policy. Those platform processes are separate from the Game’s locally stored progress.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Valve Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/)

## Children

The Game does not intentionally request personal information from children. The intended audience and content rating are determined by the relevant storefront listing.

## Changes to this policy

If the Game’s features or data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.

## Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, use the **developer contact email listed on the Game’s Google Play store page**. A direct support email address will be added here once confirmed.
